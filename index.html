Privacy Policy — Shehimo

Effective date: July 6, 2026
App: Shehimo (iOS and Android)
Developer: George Tarzi
Contact: tarztech@gmail.com

Shehimo does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works on your device and needs no account or sign-in. It has no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

The app works on your device

The prayers, services, glossary, liturgical calendar, and the included chant recordings are bundled inside the app. Reading, praying, searching, saving favorites, and following the calendar all happen entirely on your device. Nothing you read, search for, or save leaves your device.

Optional chant downloads

Shehimo includes a starter set of chant recordings in the app. As an optional convenience, you can choose to download additional recordings from the Beth Gazo collection.

  • Downloading is entirely optional — the app is fully usable without it.
  • When you tap to download a recording, the app requests that specific audio file from our server. This is a standard file download: no personal information about you is sent, there is no account, and we do not use it to identify or track you.
  • As with any request to any web server, our server may transiently record basic technical information (such as an IP address and a timestamp) for the sole purpose of delivering the file and keeping the service running and secure. This is not linked to your identity and is never used for advertising or analytics.

Audio playback

Chant and recitation play from audio files — those bundled in the app and any you have chosen to download. There is no synthetic ("text-to-speech") Syriac voice; all Syriac audio is a real recording, and texts without a recording say so plainly. Playback involves no data collection.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect analytics, usage statistics, crash logs, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or any other data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.

In-app purchases

The app offers optional "tips" to support the work. These are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see or receive your payment information. Tipping is completely optional, unlocks no personal-data collection, and gates no content — it simply supports development.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal data, it is suitable for users of any age, including children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Fonts and content

The app bundles the Serto Urhoy font by Fr. Joseph Tarzi (Meltho font license, distributed with permission), and text fonts under the SIL Open Font License. The prayer texts and chant recordings are from Fr. Joseph Tarzi's work, used with permission. All are used for on-device rendering and playback only and involve no data collection.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new effective date.
