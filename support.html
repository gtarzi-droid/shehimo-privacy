Using the app

Open Today to see the season, the day's commemoration, and the nearest hour of prayer. Browse everything in Library — the hours, services and rites, melodies, and a glossary — or filter by hour, season, and occasion. Every prayer is shown in Syriac (Serto script), English, Arabic, and transliteration; you can toggle languages and scale the type.

Chant

Where a recording exists, an item shows a player you can slow down or speed up, with an optional follow-along highlight. Items without a recording say so plainly — there is never a synthetic Syriac voice. A starter set of recordings is included; more can be downloaded optionally.

A note on accuracy

This first release is a growing, carefully prepared starter set. Texts and provisional calendar rules are being reviewed by clergy and are marked as such in the app. If you spot something that needs correcting, please email us — it helps.
